VIT PUNE — SAMPLE ACADEMIC EXAMINATION RULES
Document type: Academic policy
Status: Fictional sample document created for a RAG lab; not an official VIT Pune policy.
Version: 1.0

ATTENDANCE ELIGIBILITY
Students must have at least 75% attendance to be eligible to appear for the semester-end examination. Students whose attendance is between 65% and below 75% may submit a condonation request with supporting medical documentation. Approval is not automatic and is decided by the academic authority.

HALL TICKETS
Hall tickets are normally made available seven calendar days before the scheduled examination. Students should verify their name, seat number, and examination subjects before the examination date. Any discrepancy must be reported to the examination cell.

EXAMINATION-HALL CONDUCT
Mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth earphones, and other unauthorised electronic devices are prohibited inside the examination hall. Students must follow the invigilator's instructions. Possession of a prohibited device may be handled under the institution's examination-malpractice procedure.

RE-EVALUATION
A student may submit a re-evaluation application within seven calendar days from the date on which the result is declared. The application must be submitted through the prescribed process and the applicable fee must be paid. The exact fee is not specified in this sample document.

ABSENCE AND MISCONDUCT
Students who miss an examination should contact the examination cell and consult the applicable institutional procedure. This sample document does not specify whether a make-up examination is guaranteed, nor does it specify penalties for every category of misconduct.
