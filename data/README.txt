Important:
These are fictional sample documents designed for a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) lab.
They are not official VIT Pune rules. Do not submit them as authentic institutional policies without
replacing them with approved college documents or clearly labelling them as synthetic test data.

The assignment asks for 3–5 .txt documents, chunking, embeddings using all-MiniLM-L6-v2,
a FAISS inner-product index, retrieval, cited answers, 5 answerable questions, 3 unanswerable
questions, and a failure log. See the provided lab handout for the exact requirements.
